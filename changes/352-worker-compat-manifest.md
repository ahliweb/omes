Added the OMES worker compatibility manifest contract (`contracts/compatibility/v1/worker-compatibility-manifest.schema.json`) with valid and invalid fixtures, a replayable consumer decision table for AWCMS and OMES-Web, and `scripts/generate-worker-compat-manifest.py`, which derives a deterministic non-secret manifest from `VERSION`, the pinned `control-center/v1` worker schemas and `architecture/capabilities.json` and offers `--check` and `--verify` modes (fail closed on unreleased, unknown-schema, digest-mismatch and out-of-range input). Added synthetic enroll/poll/result/heartbeat flow tests (outbound-only, nonce, idempotency, scope mismatch, schema break, no secrets) and `docs/worker-compatibility-manifest.md` with consumer, provenance, rollover and rollback rules. Not implemented yet (tracked in #352): signing the manifest, publishing it as a tagged-release asset, a reviewed installer URI and sha256, and AWCMS builds recorded under `verified_builds`. To gate it locally, add `check-worker-compat-manifest|lint.yml|check-worker-compat-manifest|fast|blocking|generic|python3||python3 scripts/generate-worker-compat-manifest.py --check` to `scripts/ci-local.sh` and the matching step to `scripts/lint.sh`.
