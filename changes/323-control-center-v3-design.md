Added design/control-center-v3, a reference-only design proposal for the Control Center: a UX audit of the v2 prototype in ui/control-center (17 findings, including an SSH button that contradicts ADR-0027, one-click critical mutations, hard-coded counts and stale hosts shown as healthy), redesigned fleet overview, operations and approval flow, health-by-authority and on-call mobile screens, design tokens v3, and agent offices grouped by business and hierarchy (a head office approval cycle, a Hermes office whose agents walk to a delegation meeting and back to work, an Acme deployment sequence and a fleet host scan, in animated 2D and 3D views), plus a task and agenda board and avatar kit that visualize Hermes orchestration fixture data (#183); nothing in it is implemented or calls an API.
