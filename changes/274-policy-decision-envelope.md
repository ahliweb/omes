Added the policy/capability decision envelope that records and composes decisions already made by existing authorities (ADR-0032 rule 7, epic #269) without adding a policy engine, approval inbox or rule evaluation: the `contracts/observability/v1/policy-decision.schema.json` contract (tenant, correlation, actor, resource, allowlisted action name, deciding authority, closed `allow`/`deny`/`approval_required`/`unavailable` decision, machine-readable reason code, evidence freshness, opaque `approval_ref` to an existing approval, no free-text reason, command or credential field) with valid and invalid fixtures, and the stdlib-only `lib/omes/py/observability/policy_decision.py` validator and pure order-independent composer (deny beats unavailable beats approval-required beats allow; stale, unknown or expired evidence and a missing required authority are `unavailable`; mixed tenant, correlation or action is rejected; approval references are propagated unchanged and never synthesised), registered by broadening the existing `omes.observability.correlation_envelope` capability; emission by AWCMS, OMES or Hermes and use in the job runner, worker or AWCMS are not implemented yet (tracked in #274).
