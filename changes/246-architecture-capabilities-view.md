Add the OMES-side read-only `architecture-capabilities-view` Control Center contract that the future AWCMS Architecture view will consume (issue #246, part 3): `contracts/control-center/v1/architecture-capabilities-view.schema.json` projects `architecture/capabilities.json` (schema 1.1.0) and the docs/architecture.md §18.3 plane table into planes-as-lanes and capabilities-as-cards with an `implementation_status` badge and an OMES version/commit/`generated_at` provenance stamp, with `additionalProperties: false` at every object level and no raw shell/command escape hatch (registry guard C1); the shared builder lives in `lib/omes/py/architecture/capabilities_view.py`, `scripts/generate-architecture-capabilities-view.py` regenerates the checked-in fixture, and a new registry guard `AV1` in `lib/omes/py/architecture/registry.py` (run by `scripts/check-architecture.py`) fails closed whenever that fixture drifts from the registry. `docs/control-center-contracts.md` (new §2.11), `docs/architecture.md` (§16.2.1, §18.6), and `docs/control-center-and-integrations.md` (§11.1) are updated to record this as implemented while the AWCMS-side Arsitektur screen itself remains open.
