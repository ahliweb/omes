Wired the policy-decision envelope into the OMES job store (`lib/omes/py/jobs/decisions.py`, `store.submit`): OMES now emits the decisions it owns (operation allowlist, tenant scope, approval requirement with an opaque `approval_ref`, no `AI_EGRESS_*` codes) and composes them, before a job is queued, with externally supplied decisions through the existing pure composer. Any deny or unavailable wins; stale or non-live evidence, a missing required authority, an invalid decision and cross-tenant, cross-correlation or cross-action substitution fail closed (new `PolicyDeniedError` and `PolicyUnavailableError`, nothing written). Accept/deny outcomes and tenant error messages for existing requests are unchanged. The composed result and the OMES envelopes are kept as bounded `policy` evidence on the job record and a closed summary on the `submitted` audit line. OMES never creates or grants an approval and adds no policy engine or approval inbox (ADR-0032 rule 7). Not implemented yet (tracked in #358): AWCMS and Hermes emission, a request-contract field for external decisions, a re-check at `omes job run` time, and Mission Control reading the composed result.
